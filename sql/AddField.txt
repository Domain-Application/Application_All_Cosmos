Add field at All Cosmos weighbridge server (Keluli / Suasa)

1. Add Field above PKKEY at FDATA, WBDATA and DBDATA (all three, same order)
DO_QTY	decimal(10, 2)	Checked
BIN_LOCATION	nvarchar(40)	Checked

The new fields must sit directly above PKKEY (field 24 and 25, PKKEY becomes 26) in all three
tables, because the program reads the fields by position and copies lines FDATA -> WBDATA /
DBDATA at the second weight. A plain "add column" in an admin tool puts them after PKKEY,
which is wrong. Use 001_detail_add_do_qty_bin.sql in this folder instead of adding them by
hand. DO Qty (Kg) is the weight on the supplier's delivery order; Bin Location is where the
goods were put. Both are typed on the detail line (office ticket screen, or a double-click on
a line at the lane second-weight screen) and shown in the detail grid.

2. Add Field at the end of PROFILE (after "Print Counter")
API_URL	nvarchar(500)	Checked
API_USERNAME	nvarchar(50)	Checked
API_PASSWORD	nvarchar(50)	Checked
SITE_CODE	nvarchar(20)	Checked

Same as 002_profile_add_allcosmos_api.sql. Filled in on Company Profile, group AllcosmosSystem.

3. New tables AllcosmosApiRequestLog and AllcosmosApiResponseLog: run
003_allcosmos_api_logs.sql (there is nothing to add by hand).
